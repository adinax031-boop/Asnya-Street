04 / KNOW WHAT YOU MEASURE

Methodology, in the open.

Simple baselines. Explicit assumptions. Inspectable results.

Signals & execution

Trend holds long when the fast simple moving average exceeds the slow average. Mean reversion holds long when price is below its fast average. Signals computed at close t apply to the t → t+1 return. Both remain in cash during the slow-window warmup, then hold either 100% long or cash.

Costs & metrics

Each position change subtracts the one-way basis-point cost from portfolio return. No leverage, shorts, interest, dividends, market impact or terminal liquidation cost. Buy & hold starts on the same evaluation date and pays entry cost. Daily returns compound. Annualization uses 252 observations per year. Sharpe uses sample standard deviation and zero risk-free rate. Drawdown is measured from running equity peak.

Research limits

These are in-sample experiments, not proof of a trading edge. Repeated parameter selection can overfit. Validate on untouched data and model liquidity, financing, corporate actions and execution before considering real deployment. Imported data must be a daily trading series; exchange calendars are not inferred. No brokerage is connected.

Reproducibility

Built-in scenarios use seed 42. CSV exports contain date, price, position, equity, benchmark and drawdown. The ledger records parameters, dataset fingerprint and engine version. Ledger and imports use browser storage; exports are portable.
